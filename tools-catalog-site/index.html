What do these abbreviations mean?
SPA
Single-particle analysis — cryo-EM of many copies of a purified macromolecule, reconstructed into a 3D map.
cryo-ET
Cryo-electron tomography — 3D imaging of a vitrified specimen from a tilt series.
STA
Sub-tomogram averaging — aligning and averaging repeated complexes found inside tomograms.
FIB-SEM
Focused ion beam scanning electron microscopy — ion-beam milling with SEM imaging for isotropic 3D volumes.
SBEM
Serial block-face SEM — diamond-knife sectioning inside the SEM chamber, for very large 3D volumes.
ssTEM
Serial-section transmission electron microscopy — stacking TEM images of consecutive sections.
array tomo
Array tomography — serial sections collected on a substrate and re-imaged in SEM.
vEM
Volume electron microscopy — the umbrella over FIB-SEM, SBEM, ssTEM and array tomography.
SEM
Scanning electron microscopy — surface imaging with a scanned electron beam.
CLEM
Correlative light and electron microscopy — aligning fluorescence and EM of the same region.
cryo-CLEM
CLEM performed on vitrified specimens under a cryo-fluorescence stage.
iFLM
Integrated fluorescence light microscopy — fluorescence built into a cryo-FIB instrument.
LM
Light microscopy.
